Delete your Daily Kharcha account

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Daily Kharcha and log in.
  2. Go to Settings (⚙️ on Home).
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. You can export your expenses first from the same screen.

Don't have the app any more?

Email thedailykharcha.app@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Daily Kharcha account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email, password, or Google sign-in link).
  • Your family, if you own one: the members lose access to it, and expenses waiting for you on the server are deleted.
  • Your membership of someone else's family.
  • If you shared your budget with family members ("Can view my budget"), the locked copy on our server.
  • Expenses you sent that the owner hasn't collected yet.
  • Your subscription record at RevenueCat (on request; emailed requests include it).
  • When you delete in the app: all your expenses, cards, EMIs and budgets on that phone.

What is kept

  • Expenses on a phone where you're no longer logged in stay in that phone's app until it is uninstalled. We can't reach them, because they were never on our server.
  • Backup files you saved yourself (for example in Google Drive) stay where you put them.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records as the law requires (for example for tax).
Have a subscription? Deleting your account doesn't stop Google Play from charging you. Cancel it first in Google Play › Subscriptions.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: your expenses, income, budgets, cards and EMIs are stored only on your phone. We can't see them. Our server is used only for logging in and for family sharing, and family data is locked (end-to-end encrypted) on the phone before it is sent. No ads, no selling data, no tracking.

This policy explains what the Daily Kharcha app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Daily Kharcha is made by an independent developer in India. Contact: thedailykharcha.app@gmail.com.

1. Kept only on your phone

These are saved in a private file inside the app, one file per account. They are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your expenses: amounts, categories, notes, dates, how you paid, reimbursements.
  • Your income sources, budgets and spending limits.
  • Your credit cards: only a nickname, the last 4 digits, bank, network, statement and due day and colour. We never ask for or store the full card number, expiry date or CVV.
  • Your EMIs, and which card bills you marked as paid.
  • Your app settings: theme, layout, app lock, reminders.

Bank SMS you paste: if you paste a bank SMS into "Add expense", the app reads it on your phone to fill in the form, then throws the text away. It is never saved or sent. The app does not read your SMS inbox.

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. No push server is involved. App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN check; we never receive any biometric data.

3. Subscriptions and payments

After the 30-day free trial, keeping your own budget needs a subscription (₹20 a month or ₹200 a year). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your account ID (a random code, not your name or email), which plan you bought, and purchase dates and status from the store. It never receives your expenses or any other data from the app.

4. What we never do

  • We show no ads and sell no data.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't build profiles of you.
  • We don't ask for bank logins, OTPs, full card numbers, expiry dates or CVV.
  • We don't offer loans or share your data with lenders.

5. Backups and exports

Google Drive auto-backup is off unless you turn it on. When on, once a day the app saves a backup file, locked with a password you set, to a folder called "Daily Kharcha backups" in your own Google Drive. It uses Google's "drive.file" permission, so the app can only see the files it saved there, nothing else in your Drive. Google can't open the file and we never receive it. The password is kept in your phone's secure storage so backups can run by themselves. Turning it off stops new backups; files already in your Drive stay until you delete them.

Otherwise nothing is backed up automatically. If you make a backup in Settings › Backup & export, it is locked with a password only you know and saved wherever you choose (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp). We never receive it and can't open it or reset its password. Spreadsheet (CSV) exports are not locked, so share them carefully.

6. Who else is involved

  • Supabase: login and family-sharing server (section 2).
  • Google: Google sign-in (if you choose it), Google Play billing, and Google Drive (only if you turn on Drive backup; the files are locked).
  • RevenueCat: checks subscription status (section 3).

Each follows its own privacy policy. We share nothing else with anyone, unless the law requires it.

7. Keeping it safe

All connections use HTTPS. Family data is end-to-end encrypted. Your family keys are kept in your phone's secure storage. Server access is restricted so each account can only reach its own rows. No system is perfectly secure, so please also use a screen lock and the app lock.

8. How long we keep data, and deleting it

Data on your phone stays until you delete it, delete your account or uninstall the app. Server data stays while you have an account, except member expenses, which are deleted once collected, and invite codes, which expire after 7 days.

Delete your account any time in the app: Settings › Danger zone › Delete account. This deletes your login and all your server data straight away, and your data on that phone. If you no longer have the app, see how to delete your account. Subscription records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat are deleted on request, except what the law requires them to keep (for example for tax). Delete an active subscription in Google Play first, or it keeps renewing.

9. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and similar laws elsewhere) you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a complaint. Most of your data is on your phone, where you control it directly. For anything on our server, email thedailykharcha.app@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

Daily Kharcha is meant for adults (18 and over) managing household money. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
